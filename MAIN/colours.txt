================================================================================
                        SAI FABRICS GROUP - COLOR PALETTE
================================================================================

Theme: Warm Tactile Editorial / Midnight Plum & Imperial Gold

1. COLOR PALETTE DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Midnight Plum Base (Background)
  - HEX: #18111D
  - Role: Primary canvas background. Deep, luxurious dark tone offering rich contrast for textile imagery and metallic highlights.

• Surface Card Dark Plum (Container & Card Surface)
  - HEX: #221829
  - Role: Component backgrounds, card surfaces, and elevated UI elements. Gives soft depth against the base background.

• Deep Muted Plum (Border Color)
  - HEX: #2E2235
  - Role: Architectural grid lines, dividers, subtle borders, and scrollbar track details.

• Champagne Ivory (Primary Typography & Icons)
  - HEX: #FAF4E8
  - Role: Main headings, high-legibility body text, and active interactive elements. Provides soft warm contrast without harsh white glare.

• Imperial Gold (Primary Accent)
  - HEX: #D4A34F
  - Role: Primary action items, call-to-action buttons, key statistics counters, active nav indicators, and high-priority focus states.

• Warm Gold (Secondary Accent)
  - HEX: #E5B869
  - Role: Hover states, secondary highlights, badges, subtitle accents, and warm glowing borders.

• Shimmer Skeleton Gradient
  - Neutral Base HEX: #EBE6DF
  - Highlight HEX: #F5EFE6
  - Role: Skeleton loader animations and content loading placeholders.


================================================================================
                        DESIGN PHILOSOPHY & ART DIRECTION
================================================================================

1. Industrial Craftsmanship Meets Editorial Elegance
   Sai Fabrics Group represents 10+ years of digital textile printing mastery. 
   The design eschews standard, sterile corporate templates in favor of a warm, 
   editorial aesthetic reminiscent of high-end fashion lookbooks and industrial 
   design manifestos.

2. Midnight & Imperial Gold Palette
   The choice of Midnight Plum (#18111D) and Imperial Gold (#D4A34F) evokes 
   rich fabric dyes, premium craftsmanship, and luxury textile finishing. 
   Champagne Ivory text (#FAF4E8) ensures effortless readability while keeping 
   the overall visual signature organic and tactile.

3. Sharp Architectural Geometry
   All cards, buttons, and container elements feature sharp 0px border-radii 
   and clean grid alignments (`.sf-card`). This reflects precision cutting, 
   fabric weaves, and industrial textile engineering. Hover states feature 
   hard-shadow offset projections (`4px 4px 0px 0px #D4A34F`), giving an 
   tactile physical feel to interactive elements.

4. Purposeful Motion & Micro-Interactions
   Animations are executed via GSAP ScrollTrigger to reveal content naturally 
   as the user explores. Animations exist to guide the eye and highlight 
   scale (e.g. dynamic counter statistics, interactive fabric catalog filters, 
   and timeline reveals) rather than serve as mere visual noise.

5. High Contrast & High Legibility Typography
   Pairing the editorial serif 'Playfair Display' for high-impact headlines 
   with the ultra-clean sans-serif 'Plus Jakarta Sans' for structural body copy 
   creates an instant visual hierarchy that bridges industrial authority with 
   fashion-forward elegance.
