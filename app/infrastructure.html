Our 7-Step Manufacturing Pipeline

Every meter of fabric produced at Sai Fabrics Group follows strict Reliance compliance and European export inspection protocols.

01

Artwork Evaluation

High-resolution vector & raster calibration to optimize color gamut and repeat patterns.

02

Color Calibration

Spectrophotometer shade matching ensuring batch-to-batch color consistency.

03

Fabric Pre-Treatment

Chemical coating to enhance dye absorption, brightness, and wash fastness.

04

Digital Printing

High-speed printing on 14 specialized industrial engines using eco-certified dyes.

05

Fixation & Washing

High-temperature steaming & wash-off to seal color molecules permanently.

06

100% Lightbox Audit

Continuous manual inspection under standard daylight D65 lamps prior to rolling.

07

Export Packaging

Moisture-proof poly wrapping with batch identification tags for global dispatch.
