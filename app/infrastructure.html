Our 7-Step Manufacturing Process

Every meter of fabric printed at Sai Fabrics Group follows strict Reliance rules and European export quality standards.

01

Artwork Evaluation

High-resolution file setup to optimize color depth and repeat patterns.

02

Color Calibration

Spectrophotometer shade matching to ensure batch-to-batch color consistency.

03

Fabric Pre-Treatment

Substrate coating to improve dye absorption, color brightness, and wash fastness.

04

Digital Printing

High-speed printing on 14 specialized digital lines using eco-certified inks.

05

Fixation & Washing

High-temperature steaming and washing to fix color molecules into fabric.

06

100% Lightbox Audit

Manual inspection under standard daylight D65 lamps prior to rolling.

07

Export Packaging

Moisture-proof poly wrapping with batch tags for secure transport.

EU

Export Certified

Fully compliant with European REACH Annex XVII and Reliance compliance norms.
