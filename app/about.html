// OUR STORY

From 7 Team Members in 2021 to 80+ Skilled Workers Today

Sai Fabrics Group (SAI DIGITEX) was founded with a clear goal: to provide garment manufacturers, export houses, and fashion brands with reliable digital fabric printing that delivers accurate colors and dependable delivery times.

Co-founder Dharmendra Singh brings over 18 years of hands-on textile mill printing experience. Under his leadership, the company grew from a 7-person team in 2021 into a high-capacity facility running 14 digital printing lines in Vareli, Surat.

"Our goal is not just to print fabrics, but to be a trusted production partner that supports our clients' growth."

— Dharmendra Singh & Ravindra Singh (Founders)
